Frequently asked questions

Your questions, my answers

How much does a call-out cost?

Every job comes with a free, detailed quote before work starts. You know the exact price in advance, with no hidden costs.

Can you come quickly for a fault?

For a fault, I do my best to come the same day or the next. Call directly, it's the quickest way.

Is your work guaranteed?

Yes. All my jobs are covered by a 10-year building warranty and professional liability insurance. The certificates are sent on request.

Do I need a Consuel certificate after a renovation?

After a full renovation, a certificate of compliance approved by Consuel is usually required. I prepare the file and help you all the way.

Do you install chargers in residential buildings?

Yes. I am IRVE qualified and I also handle the exchanges with the building manager for the installation in your parking space.

Do you work for businesses?

Yes: shops, offices, practices and restaurants. I adapt the working hours so as not to disrupt your business.
